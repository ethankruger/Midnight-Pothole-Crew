Who We Are

The Midnight Pothole Crew is a community-powered, after-hours road repair group based in Eau Claire, Wisconsin. We work under the radar to patch dangerous potholes when city action is too slow. No politics. No permits. Just action.

Why Join?

  • Make a real impact in your neighborhood
  • Be part of a low-key, high-effect crew
  • No experience needed, just willingness to help
